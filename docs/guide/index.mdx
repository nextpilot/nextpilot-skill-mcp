这份指南按「认识 → 编写 → 交付 → 使用」的顺序组织，每一页都可以单独阅读。

</Zh>

<En>

NextPilot Skill collects AI skills and MCP servers for flight control, with a deterministic
log-analysis service built in. This guide runs from concepts to authoring, shipping, and use —
each page stands on its own.

</En>

## 这份指南包含什么 | What this guide covers

- <Zh><strong>核心概念</strong>——Skill 与 MCP 分别是什么，如何协作。</Zh><En><strong>Core concepts</strong> — what a Skill and an MCP server each are, and how they fit together.</En>
- <Zh><strong>编写与开发</strong>——把一个想法写成可复现的能力，并暴露成可调用的工具。</Zh><En><strong>Authoring</strong> — turn an idea into a reproducible capability and expose it as callable tools.</En>
- <Zh><strong>提交与审核</strong>——材料清单、许可证与安全边界。</Zh><En><strong>Submission</strong> — required material, licensing, and safety boundaries.</En>
- <Zh><strong>下载与使用</strong>——把它接入你自己的客户端。</Zh><En><strong>Use</strong> — wire it into your own client.</En>

## 两条主线 | Two tracks

<Zh>

社区内容与平台服务是两条不同的主线：**Skill / MCP 免费开放**，用于交流和引流；
**日志分析服务是平台内置能力**，不作 Skill 分发，壁垒在检查规则、阈值逻辑与故障模式知识。

</Zh>

<En>

Community content and platform services are two separate tracks. **Skills and MCP servers are
free and open** — they exist for exchange and reach. The **log-analysis service is built in**,
not distributed as a Skill; its value is in the check rules, thresholds, and failure-mode knowledge.

</En>

> <Zh>日志全程留在你的浏览器里解析，原始 `.ulg` 不会上传服务器。</Zh><En>Logs are parsed entirely in your browser — the raw `.ulg` is never uploaded.</En>
